📌 Graph Interpretation

AQI (Air Quality Index): Overall air pollution level. Higher value = worse air quality.

PM2.5: Fine particles that enter lungs and cause breathing problems.

NO2: Harmful gas from vehicles and industries affecting respiratory system.

🟢 Low values: Safe for outdoor activities

🟡 Medium values: Sensitive people should be careful

🔴 High values: Unhealthy air, avoid outdoor exposure
